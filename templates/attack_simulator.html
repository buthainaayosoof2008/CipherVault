What is a Caesar Cipher?

A Caesar cipher replaces each letter with another letter a fixed number of positions away in the alphabet. Because there are only 25 meaningful shifts, the key space is very small.

What is Brute Force?

Brute force means systematically trying possible keys until a meaningful result is found. This simulation demonstrates why a small key space can make a cipher vulnerable to exhaustive testing.

Educational safety note: This simulator operates only on user-provided classical cipher text. It is designed to demonstrate cryptographic concepts and the weakness of small key spaces. It does not attack real accounts, systems, networks, or services.
